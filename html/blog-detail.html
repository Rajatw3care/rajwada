Real Wedding: A Destination Celebration at Lalgarh Palace, Bikaner

12 Aug 2026 By Rajwada Events Real Weddings

Home/Blogs/Lalgarh Palace Wedding

When Meera and Arjun first walked through the sandstone courtyards of Lalgarh Palace, they knew it was where they wanted to begin their story. Over three days in July, Rajwada Events turned that courtyard — and the palace's gardens, lawns and banquet halls — into the setting for one of our most memorable celebrations yet.

Day One: Mehendi & Haldi

The celebrations opened with a sunlit Mehendi in the palace's inner garden, styled in marigold, magenta and gold. Our décor team dressed the lawn with hanging floral chandeliers and low seating, while our in-house Mehendi artists worked through the afternoon. The Haldi that followed was kept intimate — close family only, turmeric paste, dhol beats, and a lot of laughter.

"We didn't want it to feel like a checklist of rituals. We wanted every moment to feel like us — and Rajwada Events understood that from day one." — Meera, the bride

Day Two: Sangeet Night

As the sun went down, the palace lawn transformed into a stage. Our team built a custom performance area with layered lighting, a live band, and a choreography schedule that let every generation of the family take the floor. The night closed with a surprise couple's performance that had two hundred guests on their feet.

Décor highlights

  • A 40-foot floral backdrop in blush, ivory and antique gold
  • Reclaimed haveli lanterns lining the guest pathway
  • A live charcoal-portrait artist stationed near the lounge seating

Day Three: Baraat, Pheras & Reception

The groom's Baraat arrived on a decorated mare, escorted by a full brass band and dhol players, drawing guests out of the palace halls and into the courtyard. The Mangal Pheras were conducted at sunset around a mandap framed in white florals and warm fairy lights, followed by a reception dinner set beneath the palace's illuminated façade.

By the end of the night, what stayed with us wasn't just the scale of the celebration — it was how personal it still felt, right down to the small details Meera and Arjun had asked us to include: her grandmother's wedding song during the Pheras, and his college friends leading the Baraat drumline.

Vendors & Partners

This celebration was brought to life with our trusted partners in photography, floral design, catering and live entertainment — all coordinated end-to-end by the Rajwada Events team.
